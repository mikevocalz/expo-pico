PICO Spatial SDK 6.2.8 made hand, mesh and plane tracking runtime grants, so the manifest entry alone no longer turns them on. `handTracking: true` now also declares `com.picovr.permission.HAND_TRACKING`. The new `spatialData` option declares `com.picovr.permission.SPATIAL_DATA` for spatial anchors, and `sceneMesh` and `sceneUnderstanding` declare it too because neither works without it. On PICO builds the root registered with `registerImmersiveScene()` asks for each declared permission when it mounts in the immersive activity, after eye tracking and one dialog at a time. `capabilities.hand.enable()` asks for `HAND_TRACKING` and the `capabilities.scene.*` calls ask for `SPATIAL_DATA` before they call native. Requests go through `requestImmersivePermission`, so a call from the 2D panel does not open a dialog and is retried later. Each permission is asked for at most once per process, never on the `quest` or `mobile` flavor, and a denial is logged. `ensureImmersivePermissions(mode)` is exported for immersive roots that `registerImmersiveScene()` did not register, along with `ensureHandTrackingPermission`, `ensureSpatialDataPermission`, `PICO_HAND_TRACKING_PERMISSION` and `PICO_SPATIAL_DATA_PERMISSION`.
